Action

Example I licensed

CloseChase: Document Collection for Bookkeeping Firms

CloseChase lets a bookkeeping firm request, collect and track the documents it needs from clients through one secure upload link, and clients don't need an account.

It started as an idea sitting in our backlog. It wasn't mine originally, but I thought it could be really useful for small firms, and maybe bigger ones too, so I built it. Through the tech transfer network I met TeamOzy, a technology company out of Africa. I demoed the product, we talked through what it could become, and we built onto it together.

  • React
  • JavaScript

Live betaFree to try during the beta

The most complete of the other apps are full MVPs, each with sign-in, a real database, and every organization's data kept private:

  • DocChase. Tax season for small CPA firms: every client's status in one pipeline, a checklist of missing documents, one-click email and text reminders, and a tracker for extension deadlines.
  • EvidenceChase. Boutique consultancies request evidence from their clients' teams, who upload it through a secure link without logging in.
  • SurveyStack. Land surveying firms take a parcel inquiry from quote to accepted job, logging municipal contacts and referral sources along the way.
  • DeepWell. Water well service companies see emergencies first, with each well's full history and overdue follow-ups on one dashboard.
  • TurnoverOps. Short-term rental hosts assign cleaners, cleaners confirm and upload proof photos, and the dashboard flags today's turnovers most at risk of falling through.

For earlier ideas, I built quick, clickable prototypes with sample data so we could see what a product might look like, like inspection tools for fire doors and warehouse mezzanines, and CRMs for dental practices and specialty contractors.

Result

CloseChase is licensed to TeamOzy, which has run it ever since. It's live in beta and free to try while the beta runs.

What it taught me: Listen to the people you're building for, then turn their vision into something that's uniquely theirs and useful.
